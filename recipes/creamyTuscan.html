Creamy Tuscan

Description

Creamy Tuscan chicken is a rich one-pan dish featuring seared chicken simmered in a creamy sauce with spinach, sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, and Parmesan cheese.

It is typically served over pasta, mashed potatoes, or with crusty bread to soak up the sauce.

Ingredients:

  • Boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • Heavy cream
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Sun-dried tomatoes
  • Fresh garlic
  • Yellow onion
  • Baby spinach
  • Italian seasoning
  • Olive oil
  • Kosher salt
  • Black pepper

Steps to Make Creamy Tuscan Chicken:

  1. Prepare the Chicken
    • Season the chicken breasts with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
    • Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
    • Sear the chicken breasts for about 5-7 minutes on each side until golden brown. Remove from the skillet and set aside.
  2. Make the Sauce
    • In the same skillet, add the minced garlic and chopped onion. Sauté until the onion is translucent.
    • Add the sun-dried tomatoes and cook for another minute.
    • Pour in the heavy cream and stir to combine, scraping any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet.
    • Gradually add the Parmesan cheese, stirring until the sauce thickens.
  3. Combine and Serve
    • Return the seared chicken to the skillet, nestling it into the sauce. Let it simmer for about 5-10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce is creamy.
    • Stir in the baby spinach until wilted.
    • Serve the creamy Tuscan chicken over pasta, rice, or with crusty bread, garnished with additional Parmesan cheese if desired.

This dish is quick to prepare and perfect for a comforting meal!
